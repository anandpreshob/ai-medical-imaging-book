## Displayed replacements (2026-10-11)

The four SVG entries above are archived original diagrams. Chapter 11 displays generated PNGs with the same stems: projection-geometry.png, evaluation-splits.png, triage-workload.png, and agent-workflow.png. These were made with the built-in image generation tool. Their prompts and accepted revisions are recorded in [workflow-imagegen-prompts.md](../workflow-imagegen-prompts.md). The projection illustration is qualitative, not exact ray geometry; partition images are synthetic; workload counts remain hypothetical; the workflow is proposed, not a cleared product. The two real radiographs are unchanged. SVG regeneration does not overwrite these PNGs.
